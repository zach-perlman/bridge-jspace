Write your answers under "Dan:" BEFORE these results are opened. Spec and rule: [`jobs/c3/X0.md`](../jobs/c3/X0.md) (commit named when these answers are committed). X0 runs only after these answers are committed.

**Plain words.** Fresh scenes, a seed never opened: "Alice picked gamma. ... The locker of gamma is nine. ..." The asked person's pick is changed (gamma → epsilon). At the bridge's word, at one layer, we paste the change three ways:
- whole;
- without its J++ part (25 atoms);
- without a random part of the same share.

A lookup ("Which locker belongs to Alice's pick?") and a recall ("Which position in the Greek alphabet does Alice's pick have?") read the identical patched context.

The measures:
- J-dependence (Jdep) = the share of switches lost without the J++ part.
- Rdep = the same without the random part.
- excess = Jdep − Rdep.

**The rule** (fixed 23 Oct). On the chat template at the predicted layer (27B L32, 9B L12):
- **confirmed** iff the lookup's excess ≤ 0.10 and the recall's Jdep ≥ 0.30;
- **killed** iff the lookup's excess ≥ 0.20 or the recall's Jdep < 0.20;
- **can't tell** if the whole change switches < 30% of either reader there.
