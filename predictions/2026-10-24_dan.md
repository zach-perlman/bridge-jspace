## Batch 25 (24 Oct): is C3's split real, and is it about J++? (X0)

Spec and rule: [`jobs/c3/X0.md`](../jobs/c3/X0.md), with amendment 1 (24 Oct, before any data). The commit is named when these answers are committed. X0 may run before then (Dan, 24 Oct: no overnight slot), but no X0 result is opened until these answers are committed.

**Plain words.** The data are fresh scenes from a seed never opened, e.g. "Alice picked gamma. ... The locker of gamma is nine. ..." The asked person's pick is changed (gamma → epsilon). At the bridge's word, at one layer, the change is pasted in three ways:
- whole;
- without its J++ part (25 atoms);
- without a random part of the same share.

A lookup ("Which locker belongs to Alice's pick?") and a recall ("Which position in the Greek alphabet does Alice's pick have?") read the identical patched context.

**The read.** The verdict uses the top-token switch: the top token over the whole vocabulary is the alt answer's first token.
- Chat: read after "Answer:" plus a space token, as on 23 Oct.
- Raw: the first non-whitespace token the model writes after "Answer:".
- The cand read (alt first among the digits) is descriptive only.

**The measures:**
- J-dependence (Jdep) = the share of switches lost without the J++ part;
- Rdep = the same without the random part;
- excess = Jdep − Rdep.

Each dictionary's random part matches that dictionary's own share of the change.

**The rule.** On the chat template at the predicted layer (27B L32, 9B L12):
- **confirmed** if the lookup's excess ≤ 0.10 and the recall's Jdep ≥ 0.30 (fixed 23 Oct);
- **killed** if the lookup's excess ≥ 0.20 or the recall's Jdep < 0.20 (added 24 Oct, before data);
- **not confirmed** otherwise (added 24 Oct);
- **can't tell** if, for either reader, the whole change switches < 30% of items or fewer than 30 items (added 24 Oct).

Labels per reader: **around** (excess ≤ 0.10, upper bound ≤ 0.20), **through** (Jdep ≥ 0.30, excess ≥ 0.20, lower bound ≥ 0.10), else **mixed**.

The same test also runs:
- in raw text ("... Question: ... Answer:");
- with three other dictionaries in place of J++: W_U (the logit lens's directions), W_E (input embeddings) and random.
