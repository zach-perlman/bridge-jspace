# Finding 7: the layer-gap test and the layer-16 embedding test (Qwen3.6-27B)

Draft for the README, not README.md. UNVERIFIED (exploration phase). Every number below is copied from the two analysis files named under "Key numbers". Both were written by the committed analysis stage (`scripts/early_push_where.py analyze --rule v2`, script as of c2add04, which is also what the runs used for these rules), and both have the same content as the summaries the runs wrote themselves.

## What was tested

The test used 100 new scenes (fresh draw, seed 2, none taken from map_A8_v1 or d4_where_fresh_27b), with the four families built on the same scenes and only items the model answers right unedited. Four interventions were applied at the stated bridge's word and at its whole sentence, every second layer from 12 to 34: a push along the lens's word difference (4x and 8x the scene's reference norm), a push of the same norms along the input-embedding difference W_E[X] − W_E[I], the full patch of the residual taken from the prompt whose sentence names the other bridge (at its own size), and a random push. The measure is the share of lookups (prompt → prompt) that switch to the other bridge's answer.

Layer-gap question (jobs/d4/d4_layer_gap_v2_27b.yaml, rule fixed before the run): does the full patch keep moving lookups after the lens push stops? An arm "stops" at a layer when every valid cell there has a Wilson upper bound below 5%. It "keeps moving" when some valid cell has a lower bound above 5%. H-gap holds iff the patch stops at least 6 layers after the lens push and is still moving at the lens push's stop layer. H-no-gap holds iff the two stops are at most 2 layers apart.

Embedding question (d4_embed_27b, jobs/d4/d4_embed_27b.yaml): is the layer-16 lens push like editing the input word? This run uses the same 100 scenes, the word only, layers 12-24 and scales 1/2/4/8. It compares the lens push with an input-embedding push of equal norm and decides between H_lens and H_input with `embed_test`.

## Verdicts by the committed rules

Layer gap: **H-gap**, at the word and at the sentence alike. The lens push stops at layer 20 and the full patch at layer 28, a gap of +8 layers. At layer 20 the patch still switches 65% of lookups at the word and 69% at the sentence. The embedding arm never moves a lookup, so its stop is the first grid layer (12). The random arm switches no lookup at any layer or scale.

Embedding test: **no lead**. The rule's wording is "the lens push moves ≤ 5% of lookups at layers 16-18", so neither H_lens nor H_input is reached. The rule pools all valid cells at layers 16 and 18 across scales 1, 2, 4 and 8. The lens push moves lookups only at 8x, so the pooled rate is 30/800 = 3.75%. The rule checks no-lead first. Without that ordering, d_lens's lower bound (above 0) would have given H_lens. H_input fails either way, because d_input's upper bound is below 0. The line "Verdict: inconclusive" at the top of that run's summary.md is the D4 where-rule at the cell (word, layer 16, 8x), not this test.

## What it means for finding 7

From layer 20 on, the results fit finding 7 ("a lens push at the stated sentence writes recall, not the lookup") and sharpen it. At the stated sentence the lens push writes recall and moves no lookup. Yet the residual there still carries what the lookup uses: the full patch moves 65-69% of lookups at layer 20 and 24-50% at layer 26, and only stops at layer 28. So between layers 20 and 26, the lookup's bridge sits in the residual at the stated sentence, but outside the part the lens push writes.

Before layer 20, whether the early lookup movement is an input edit stays open. The lens push moves 9-19% of lookups at layers 14-18 (at 8x only), and the embedding push moves none. But the embedding push also moves no recall, at any layer or scale, where the lens push moves 83% and the patch 100%. Injected mid-stack at these norms, the input-embedding direction is inert. It therefore fails its own positive control and cannot tell whether the layer-16 effect looks like an input edit. The full patch is the input edit's own change at these positions: at layer 16 it moves 85% of lookups against the lens push's 19%. The committed "no lead" verdict comes from pooling over scales where nothing moves, not from an absent effect. Finding 7's caveat ("at layer 16 the lookup moves too, which may be an input edit") should stay as written: these two runs neither confirm it nor remove it. A test that could settle it (proposal, for Dan to decide): split the layer-16 patch at the word into the part along the lens's read directions and the rest, as D1 does, and ask which part moves the lookup.

## Weak spots

There is one model, one draw of 100 scenes and one random-direction seed. The two runs share these scenes, so their identical lens rows are not a replication. The patch has the residual's own size and is not norm-matched to the pushes, so part of "the patch moves more" may be dose. The lens push moves lookups at one scale only (8x). At the word, its 8x cells from layer 20 fail the off-path check, so its stop rests on the 4x cells (the 8x cells also read 0/100). The embedding arm is inert, so it is not a working control.
