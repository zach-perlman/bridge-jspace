### Cross-figure patterns

- 95% interval key phrase, seven forms: "whiskers: 95% interval" (fig04, fig06, fig07, fig08a, fig12), "[ ] 95% interval" (fig01), "[95%]" (fig05, appB_f12 sub, appB_f17 sub), "95% whiskers" (figA1), "bands and whiskers: 95%" (appB_f15), "whiskers: 95%" (appB_f17), "95% interval" as a column sub (appB_f14). Whiskers drawn with no phrase at all: fig09, fig10, fig11, fig13, appB_f9, appB_f12, appB_f8_facts, appB_f8_directions, appB_f18, appB_f17 heads.
- Band phrase: "within 0.10 of a random part" (fig01, appB_f8_*) vs "within 0.10 of random" (appB_f12); the band reused for "range of 200 random vectors" (fig08b).
