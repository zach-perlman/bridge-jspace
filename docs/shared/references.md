# References

Works the root README and the two reports cite. Links were checked on 30 Oct 2026.

## The lens

- Gurnee et al. 2026. *Verbalizable Representations Form a Global Workspace in Language Models.* Transformer Circuits Thread; arXiv 2607.15495. https://transformer-circuits.pub/2026/workspace/index.html. Code for the J-lens and J++: https://github.com/anthropics/jacobian-lens

## Recall, binding and lookup

- Geva, Bastings, Filippova and Globerson 2023. *Dissecting Recall of Factual Associations in Auto-Regressive Language Models.* EMNLP 2023 (subject enrichment: a subject's attributes are written into its last token before the question needs them).
- Feng and Steinhardt 2023. *How do Language Models Bind Entities in Context?*
- Dai et al. 2024. Binding in a low-dimensional order subspace.
- Prakash et al. 2025. Lookback pointers: models track entities through pointers to their earlier mention.
- Gur-Arieh et al. 2025. How models retrieve bound entities in context (the identity and position routes).
- Kawada and Kellis 2026. *Evidence Integration in Large Language Models.* arXiv 2609.04290. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04290 (the state that arbitrates between evidence and the model's prior is not in the verbalizable workspace, in Llama).

## Methods

- Marks and Tegmark 2023. *The Geometry of Truth* (mass-mean directions).
- Makelov et al. 2023. *Is This the Subspace You Are Looking For?* (subspace patching can mislead; the reason for our random parts).
